# KOLLAZ

Hi, I am Valentin Iosif, founder of KOLLAZ in Ottawa, Canada.

KOLLAZ builds plain tools that answer real money and small business questions with real numbers. No hype, no guesses, and no signup walls in front of the answer.

## What we build

**[Kollaz Money](https://kollazmoney.com)** publishes free calculators, plain guides, and spreadsheets that show regular people in Canada and the US the date their debt ends and the move that pulls it closer. Start with the guides, run your own numbers, and see your real payoff date.

**[Kollaz Toolz](https://kollaztoolz.com)** builds free online calculators that run in your browser. Your numbers never leave your device. The most used one is [Know Your Date](https://kollaztoolz.com/know-your-date/), a free calculator that turns a balance, a rate, and a payment into the exact month your debt is gone.

**[Website Rescue](https://rescue.kollaztoolz.com)** helps small business owners whose WordPress sites were hacked. We clean the infection, close the door it came through, and hand over a written record of what was done, for a fixed price agreed in writing before work starts.

## Working principles

- Show the math. Every tool shows its numbers, not just a chart.
- Plain words. Everything customer facing is written at a grade 9 reading level.
- Privacy first. The calculators run in the browser, so personal numbers stay on the visitor's own machine.
- Fixed prices in writing before work starts on any service.
